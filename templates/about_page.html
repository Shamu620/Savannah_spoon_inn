ABOUT THE SAVANNAH SPOON INN

“Where every plate tells a story.”

Our Story

Savannah Spoon Inn began with a simple belief: a good meal does more than fill a plate. It brings people together. We built our kitchen around the warmth of the East African table, where food is cooked slowly, served generously and always shared.

Every dish on our menu carries a story, from the smoke of a flame-seared grill to the sweetness of a spiced Swahili tea. We cook with fresh local ingredients and bold spices, and we mix in flavours from across the world, so there is something for every guest at the table.

Whether you are here for a quick coffee, a family feast or a long evening with friends, we want you to step in, sit back and feel at home.
